_A segment from an Itānish origin myth detailing the creation of the world. Told by Geghdol Zhilkazna=dan Uzāgid of the Northern Itān Tribes, 93TOR._

"Ysh knew the battle between good and evil would continue, and she gouged her eyeballs out to give birth to two sons, *Yolmu* and *Lyd*. Upon the birth of the two moons above the mirror lake, their reflection begot two daughters of Ghadoz who also emerged from the lake:  Ulla ("cruelty") and Blyz ("thievery"). Ysh, Yolmu, and Lyd could see that the mirror lake was a thing of evil, and buried it under a large pile of earth, the pile which became the Black Wall mountain ranges."
