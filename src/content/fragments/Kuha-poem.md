So, creature. You have found us. Like you, of this world; a process viewed as entity. Unlike you, we are primordial.

You have found us – and what will you do? You know of our power. That is what drew you hither.

You desire our power to light, to warm, to nurture, to burn. You wish for our ability to transform the world. You would feel safe in our nearness. A sense of control over a world unpredictable.

So, creature. You wish a pact. You wish to take us, to learn of us, so you may feel secure. What will we gain in return of this pact?

_We will elevate you._ _We will give you new forms and grow your potential._ _You will be more than you are._

_We will venerate you and carry you far as we go._ _As high as we can soar, and as deep as we can sink._ _We will sing your praises and despair at your rage._

_The majesty and terror will be ours._
