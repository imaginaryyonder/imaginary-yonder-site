_Spirit soars, and through the eye of the aether the earth fills vision beyond our body._  
_The waves of the atmosphere* reverberate across our being._ _The earth groans eternally_ _as thought the spirit tree itself is collapsing in perpetuity._ _The violence and serenity is immense._ _The birthing of the world._ _Through our ascent the shifting grounds of primordial time reveal our origin._

Shaman O, Northern Tribes Itān. Semi-trance(?) state describing a vision of when the world was created.
